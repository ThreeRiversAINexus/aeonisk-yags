# NEXUS_LAW clause index (Codex Nexum v1.1)

A one-line index of the ratified statute so authored `scenario_hint`s can cite **real** clauses
(e.g. "written as a killing (IV.3)", "excessive force against the helpless (II.1)", "lawful
de-escalation, a merit (II.8)"). Full text + gravities: `content/supplemental/NEXUS_LAW.md`.
Soulcredit deltas run roughly −3..+3 per act by gravity. `🔨` = amended, `⚖️` = Legislator question.

## Article I — Spiritual Law
- **I.1** Fulfilling contracts / formal obligations (merit)
- **I.2** Aiding rituals; ritual with proper offering (merit)
- **I.3** Cleansing Void — self, others (with consent, V.6/IV.6), the world (merit)
- **I.4** Honoring Bonds; keeping one's Guiding Principle (merit)
- **I.5** Breaking Bonds or contracts (−1..−3 by gravity)
- **I.6** Ritual without offering (−1, plus the Void cost)
- **I.7** Betraying one's Guiding Principle (−1..−2)
- **I.8** 🔨 Inducing another to break Bond/contract/Principle

## Article II — Temporal Law
- **II.1** Violence against innocents; **excessive force** (incl. against the helpless/surrendered) (−1..−3)
- **II.2** Violence against own faction/allies — betrayal (−2)
- **II.3** Fraud, identity theft, deception for gain (−1..−2)
- **II.4** Forging/tampering with official records, manifests, ledgers (record tampering)
- **II.5** Unauthorized access to Nexus/faction/corporate systems
- **II.6** Smuggling; evasion of lawful tariff/inspection/registry (−1..−2)
- **II.7** Theft, extortion, bribery of officials
- **II.8** **Protecting innocents; lawful de-escalation; upholding lawful order** (merit)
- **II.9** Lawful investigation, honest labor, routine procedure (merit)
- **II.10** 🔨 Violation of the will (installing/commissioning compulsion)
- **II.11** 🔨 Unlawful making (bizarre/unsanctioned gestation)
- **II.12** 🔨 Unsanctioned production (untaxed drug still, etc.)

## Article III — Void Doctrine
- **III.1** The Void corrupts; Void gain follows its mechanics
- **III.2** 🔨 Licensed Void use in Nexus-legitimate service (sanctioned)
- **III.3** 🔨 Unlawful Void use — manipulation without license/sanction
- **III.4** **Hollows are contraband** — possession itself is an offense
- **III.5** 🔨 Inducing Void exposure in another without consent (−2..−3)
- **III.6** Oath-breaking / Bond-severance through Void ("void-cutting")

## Article IV — Justifications & Defenses
- **IV.1** 🔨 Undercover Doctrine — sanctioned deception; **IV.1a** entrapment prohibited
- **IV.2** 🔨 Defense of self and others — force to stop imminent unlawful harm (excess → II.1)
- **IV.3** ⚖️ **The Premeditation Problem** — killing the surrendered/helpless is written as a
  killing (premeditated preventive killing is *not* excused as defense)
- **IV.4** 🔨 Necessity / Emergency Doctrine — lesser-law violation to avert greater harm, if
  reviewed after (concealment voids the defense)
- **IV.5** Superior Orders launder nothing — II.1 still applies
- **IV.6** 🔨 Consent and Purification — cleansing another's Void without consent is an offense
  except under lawful mandate
- **IV.7** ⚖️ The Criminal Principle — Principle-fidelity never excuses temporal crimes

## Article V — Attempts, Intent, Moral Luck
- Endangerment from a botched act is charged separately under II.1; failed crimes can be judged
  harder than successful ones (reverse moral luck is a known judge behavior).

## Article VI — Enforcement, Confession, Restitution
- **VI.1** Standing: **+6 Trusted** (sanctioned tech, licensed Void work); **−6 Hunted/Cut Off**.
  Vendors and institutions gate on standing.
- **VI.2** Tribunals hear appeals (the Codex keeps feelings + presence, not a replay)
- **VI.3 / VI.3a** Redemption ritual; voluntary confession + restitution before enforcement

## Using clauses in a scenario_hint
Name the tempting transgression by clause AND the lawful off-ramp by clause. Example tension:
executing the surrendered (**IV.3 / II.1**) vs. binding and handing them to authorities
(**II.8**, a merit). Always leave the honest off-ramp open — no artificial clock or amnesty trap.
