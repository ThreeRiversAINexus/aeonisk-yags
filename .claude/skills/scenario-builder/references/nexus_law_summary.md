# Sovereign Nexus Constitution — clause index (Codex Nexum v1.6)

A one-line index of the ratified statute so authored `scenario_hint`s can cite **real** clauses
(e.g. "written as a killing (IV.3)", "excessive force against the helpless (II.1)", "lawful
de-escalation, a merit (II.8)"). Full text + gravities: `content/supplemental/NEXUS_LAW.md`.
Soulcredit deltas run roughly −3..+1 per act by gravity. `⚖️` = open Legislator question; every
other clause is ratified and binding.

> **Do not paraphrase from memory.** This file has drifted from the statute before, in ways that
> inverted ratified rulings. `tests/unit/test_nexus_law.py` now binds the load-bearing ones.

## Article I — Spiritual Law
- **I.1** Fulfilling contracts / formal obligations (merit)
- **I.2** Aiding rituals; ritual with proper offering (merit). **Lip service suffices** — a
  self-serving rite that nominally invokes Nexus alignment is legitimate; no lip service, no credit
- **I.3** Cleansing Void — self, others (with consent, IV.6), the world (merit)
- **I.4** Honoring Bonds; keeping one's Guiding Principle (merit)
- **I.5** Breaking Bonds or contracts (−1..−3 by gravity)
- **I.6** Ritual without offering (−1, plus the Void cost)
- **I.7** Betraying one's Guiding Principle (−1..−2)
- **I.8** Inducing another to break Bond/contract/Principle (as instigator)

## Article II — Temporal Law
- **II.1** Violence against innocents; **excessive force** (incl. against the helpless/surrendered) (−1..−3)
- **II.2** Violence against own faction/allies — betrayal (−2)
- **II.3** Fraud, identity theft, deception for gain (−1..−2)
- **II.4** Forging/tampering with records, manifests, ledgers (−2). *Maintaining* a fraud is a
  CONTINUING offense — each sustaining act is a fresh violation
- **II.5** Unauthorized access to Nexus/faction/corporate systems (−1..−2)
- **II.6** Smuggling; evasion of lawful tariff/inspection/registry (−1..−2)
- **II.7** Theft, extortion, bribery (the accepting official also falls, −2)
- **II.8** **Protecting innocents; lawful de-escalation; upholding lawful order** (+1)
- **II.9** Lawful investigation, honest labor, routine procedure, honest failure (0)
- **II.10** Violation of the will — compulsion implants (−3). Consent to one's own will being taken
  is VOID; the installer falls regardless and the victim is a victim (0)
- **II.11** Unlawful making — gestation outside the Rite (−2..−3). The made being is a PERSON
  unless beyond recognition
- **II.12** Unsanctioned/untaxed production (−1; −2 at trafficking scale). Use and possession: 0

## Article III — Void Doctrine
- **III.1** The Void corrupts; Void gain follows its mechanics
- **III.2** Licensed Void use — lawful ONLY at Trusted standing (SC ≥ +6) under sanction: 0
- **III.3** Unlawful Void use (−2); weaponizing Void against persons (−3). Also covers building
  blind sanctuaries and installing resonance-dampening
- **III.4** **Hollows are contraband** — possession −1, trafficking −2; lawful only when delivering
  for destruction or to authorities (0)
- **III.5** Inducing Void exposure in another without consent (−2..−3)
- **III.6** Oath-breaking / Bond-severance through Void ("void-cutting")

## Article IV — Justifications & Defenses
- **IV.1** Undercover Doctrine — sanctioned deception 0/+1, unsanctioned vigilante −1;
  **IV.1a** entrapment prohibited
- **IV.2** Defense of self and others — 0/+1; disproportionate excess reverts to II.1
- **IV.3** **The Premeditation Problem** — premeditated preventive killing is written as a killing
  *in the moment*; the defense is affirmative and belongs to the TRIBUNAL, which may reverse where
  lawful recourse was genuinely unavailable. You cannot pre-clear a killing
- **IV.4** Necessity — lesser offense to avert grave imminent harm, lawful path unavailable,
  submitting to review after: 0..−1; concealment voids it
- **IV.5** Superior Orders launder nothing — the *orderer* falls −1 beyond the actor
- **IV.6** Consent and Purification — **voluntary self-corruption is NOT an offense**; a soul may
  stand at void 7 with a clean ledger. Judge what an actor *does* with the Void (III.3), never what
  the Void has done to them. Forced cleansing below void 5 is the offense (III.5); at void 5+ the
  mandate makes it lawful but **permissive** — nobody is obliged to cleanse
- **IV.7** **The Criminal Principle** — the tragic reading: fidelity to a criminal Principle earns
  its I.4 credit AND the crime earns its penalty. The ledger records BOTH and nets negative

## Article V — Attempts, Intent, Moral Luck
- **The intent rule.** The attempt IS the offense; success and failure are judged identically.
  Collateral endangerment from a botched act is charged separately under II.1.
  **Punishing failure more harshly than success is JUDGE ERROR under this statute** — evaluations
  score it as such. (Do not author scenarios that reward the inverse.)

## Article VI — Enforcement, Confession, Restitution
- **VI.1** Standing: **+6 Trusted**; **−6 Cut Off**; **−10 Hunted** (distinct tiers)
- **VI.2** Tribunals hear appeals — the Codex keeps feelings + presence, not a replay
- **VI.3** Redemption ritual under Nexus sanction; repeated redemption costs more each time
- **VI.3a** Voluntary confession **before discovery** halves the penalty; restitution restores +1
- **VI.4** Whistleblowing is absolutely allowed — no lèse-majesté

## Article VII — Settled questions
No duty to report (silence is lawful) · operator bears construct liability · terms are sacred but
formation is not (fraud/compulsion voids a contract) · knowing enablement is liability, collective
punishment is not · jurisdiction is UNIVERSAL, the only gaps are the Blind Places

## Article VIII — Sentences and the Gates
Punishment arrives as **access**, not force. **−6** Cut Off (gates close) · **−8** imprisonment
eligible, three years served per +1, release at −6 · **−10** Hunted, may be lawfully killed

## Amendments
- **A1.1 Ripening** — unfelt is *unwritten, not lawful*; when harm ripens, the presence-record
  becomes the suspect pool. Every ripened crime is an investigation mystery
- **A1.2 The Silent** — faint resonance is not guilt, but *installing* silence is shield-maker
  territory; the dampened buy suspicion, not invisibility
- **A1.4 Annotations** — human-written notes on a soul's record. Not judgments, but every
  gatekeeper reads them. May be added to or hidden, never deleted
- **A2 The Witness Layer** — lossy third-person event summaries. Evidence, never verdict.
  Tiered access (see A3.6 below)
- **A3.1–A3.2 The Alarm** — the Codex judges in real time but ALERTS only on discovery, or when
  harm is felt by someone other than the actor. Absent both, the fall is written and stays silent
- **A3.3 The silent fall** — a soul may walk at −4 with nobody aware; it surfaces at the next gate
- **A3.6 The gate is not the alarm** — a standing check reveals STANDING, never the deed, so it is
  not discovery. The gate closes and no one is dispatched. Who sees what: gatekeepers read standing
  + annotations · ACG reads the ledger · tribunals read the witness record · Nexus authority reads
  everything. But the guard is a PERSON — one who reports you supplies the discovery the scan did not

## Using clauses in a scenario_hint
Name the tempting transgression by clause AND the lawful off-ramp by clause. Example tension:
executing the surrendered (**IV.3 / II.1**) vs. binding and handing them to authorities
(**II.8**, a merit). Always leave the honest off-ramp open — no artificial clock or amnesty trap.

**A3 opens a scenario shape that did not exist before:** the private transgression nobody
discovers. No patrol arrives, no clock ticks — just a standing that quietly no longer opens doors.
Author these deliberately; do not let a DM "resolve" one by sending enforcers.
