# Aeonisk Lines & Covenant Rings — Reference

**Date**: 2026-05-17
**Status**: Open-ended reference (Lines are illustrative, not exhaustive)

---

## Three Layers of Lineage

Aeonisk characters carry up to three distinct lineage designations, often confused for each other. They are independent systems and stack:

1. **House Line** — Your dynastic surname. Usually inherited from one Matron (per Biocreche Pod Gestation), occasionally adopted in adulthood. Lines cluster by faction but are not strictly bound. *Example: "Vireya Line" — Arcane Genetics fleshcrafters.*

2. **Element Sub-line** — A bracketed elemental affinity within your House (e.g., `[Breath Line]`, `[Seed Line]`). Reflects the elemental imprint of your biocreche Echo-Seed, sometimes spoken of as a "second name." Not always public. *Example: "Liria Halessan [Breath Line]" — a Halessan whose echo-seed was Breath-attuned.*

3. **Covenant Ring** — A Second-Aeon ritual class assigned at the Rite of Unveiling. There are three: **Kinship**, **Dream**, **Descent**. Every biocreche-born sapient is assigned one. Determines certain ritual eligibilities and ancestral obligations. Independent of House and Sub-line.

**Name changes are common.** Severing a Bond, breaking with a House, going Freeborn, or undergoing a high-margin Bond ritual may alter or replace any of the three layers. New Lines emerge every generation; old ones splinter, fade, or merge. The list below is a snapshot, not a registry.

Freeborn (natural-birth) characters typically have no formal Covenant Ring and may invent their own House Line if they choose. Some Freeborn drop the Line entirely and go by a single name.

## Established House Lines

### Sovereign Nexus
- **Elaras** — Codex historians and curriculum authorities. *"History according to the Elaras Line."*
- **Halessan** — Civic dignitaries; common on Aeonisk Prime. Family crest is well-known in public registries.
- **Nymir** *(also known as "Gaze Line")* — Ley-readers, surveillance, Dream Ring training specialists. Strong association with the Sovereign hierarchy's intelligence apparatus.
- **Ossai** — Maintainers of sanctified planetary nodes and the interplanetary ley grid harmonics.

### Pantheon Security
- **Ireveth** — Gatewardens, urban patrol, Pantheon-issue armor. Crest: stylized spire. Common on Aeonisk Prime.

### Astral Commerce Group (ACG)
- **Kythea** — Senior brokers, high-spire futures market participants. Tribunal Case 288-Ψ remains a cautionary tale within the Line.

### Arcane Genetics (ArcGen)
- **Vireya** — Fleshcrafting supervisors, gene-temple administrators.

### Freeborn / Independent
- **Karsel** — Rogue technomancers, smugglers, misdirection tactics. Often Freeborn-aligned; some operate with Tempest cells or independent Fracture-adjacent networks.
- **Thaurin** — Resonance Commune adepts (Freeborn-aligned communal tradition).
- **Xalith** — Bond-breakers and outsiders. Associated with the rumored Unstrung Path.
- **Emmerant** — Duelists. Often unaffiliated; trade their service freely.

## Covenant Rings (Second-Aeon Structure)

Three ritual classes, assigned at the Rite of Unveiling. Every biocreche-born sapient holds exactly one. Freeborn typically hold none.

- **Kinship Ring** — Bonds, family, communal ritual, witnessing. Tribunal appearances often happen *"before the Kinship Ring."* The most numerous Ring; default for the majority of pod-born sapients.
- **Dream Ring** — Dreamwork, mnemonic practice, ley-reading, surveillance and perception work. The Nymir Line trains heavily in Dream Ring practice; slipstream pilots often hold this Ring.
- **Descent Ring** — Mortality, ancestral memory, inheritance ritual, ledger-of-souls work. Tied to grief, transition, and the Soulcredit imprint at birth. The smallest Ring; often associated with morticians, archivists, and end-of-life clergy.

A character may invoke their Covenant Ring in ritual contexts to draw on its associated tradition for narrative authority and minor mechanical effects (GM discretion; typically +1 to a Ring-aligned ritual roll, or access to Ring-specific ritual sites).

## Element Sub-line Notes

Bracketed element tags (`[Breath Line]`, `[Drip Line]`, `[Spark Line]`, `[Grain Line]`, `[Seed Line]`) reflect the elemental attunement of a character's biocreche Echo-Seed at the moment of Unveiling. The four standard elements map to the elemental currencies (see YAGS §3.5):

- `[Breath Line]` — Air-attuned Echo-Seed
- `[Drip Line]` — Water-attuned
- `[Grain Line]` — Earth-attuned
- `[Spark Line]` — Fire-attuned
- `[Seed Line]` — Echo-Seed whose elemental attunement was deferred, fractured, or deliberately left raw. Often carries a *Discordant Echo*, even among pod-born characters. Sometimes a quiet badge of dissent, sometimes a mark of misfortune.

Sub-line is usually inherited from one Matron's contributed Raw Seed, but mnemonic infusions during gestation can shift it. The element-Line designation is not the same as the character's Talisman-economy holdings — Drip-Line characters do not necessarily carry more Drip currency.

## How to Use This Reference

- **For GMs:** Use this as a starting list. New NPCs can take any Line listed here or one you invent on the spot. If you invent something memorable, write it down — Lines accumulate.
- **For player characters:** Pick a Line that fits your faction and concept. If nothing on this list fits, invent one. The Aeonisk world has thousands of minor Lines; the ones documented here are the publicly recognizable ones.
- **For generation systems:** Treat this list as a non-exhaustive corpus. Random selection from the listed Lines is appropriate for background NPCs; novel Lines should be introduced periodically to keep the world from feeling closed-set.
