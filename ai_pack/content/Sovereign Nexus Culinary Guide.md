## 0 · Currency & Exchange

> **Breaths** – smallest unit of account; ritual offerings, small change, child's-allowance tier.
> **Drips** – everyday coin of urban markets. *(1 Drip = 20 Breaths)*
> **Grains** – ceremonial / high-value tender. Used for feasts, oaths, fine vintages, prestige firmware. *(1 Grain = 20 Drips = 400 Breaths)*
> **Sparks** – premium tech and ritual credit. Kitchen appliances, ritual-grade firmware, gene-tier services. *(1 Spark = 20 Grains = 400 Drips = 8 000 Breaths)*
> **Hollow Shards** – **contraband**; refined Void energy. Black-market exchange ≈ 50–150 Drips per Shard depending on purity. *Never exchange legally.*

*Regional variance ±20–30%; canonical hierarchy per Economy & Money-Making Guide §0. Hollow Shards never trade through Nexus-licensed venues.*

## 8 · GM Guidance

1. **Combine Effects** judiciously. Stacking multiple +1 items should rarely exceed +3 total bonus without narrative justification.
2. **Contraband** trades always carry surveillance or Void complications — perfect plot hooks.
3. **Currency rewards by tier:** A handful of **Drips** suits an evening's tip or favour repaid. A **Grain** is a notable gift — funds a celebratory meal or a low-tier firmware unlock. A **Spark** is a serious reward — funds a kitchen appliance or high-tier firmware. **Hollow Shards** are story complications first, currency second; treat any payout in Shards as a hook, not a bonus.
4. **Soulcredit interactions:** Items that confer or jeopardize Soulcredit (Promise Pudding, ChefNet blunders, prestige firmware tier-locks) should be tracked at the table; consult Economy Guide §8 for the canonical SC band table (-10 to +10).
