# Aeonisk — Play Packet v1.4.0

*Will is Power. Bond is Law. Void is Real.*

A science-fantasy tabletop RPG of contractual magic, ledgered souls, and
factions with no clean hands. Built on YAGS (Yet Another Game System).

---

## The fastest way to play: give it to an AI

Aeonisk is designed to be run by a language model. You do not need a group, a
free Saturday, or someone willing to read sixty pages of lore first.

1. Open a chat with a capable model (Claude, GPT, Gemini — anything that
   accepts file uploads and holds a long context).
2. Upload **everything in this packet**, or at minimum the six documents marked
   ★ below.
3. Paste the contents of `01-start-here/DM_SYSTEM_PROMPT.md` as your first
   message — or as a system prompt / custom instruction / project instruction
   if your tool has one.
4. It will offer you a menu. Pick **A. Quickstart** if you know nothing yet.

That's it. The prompt makes the model run as the *Codex Nexum* — the astral
computer that is the law of this setting, not merely a book about it. It rolls
in the open (`Attribute × Skill + d20`), tracks your Soulcredit and Void, and
refuses to invent canon it can't find in the documents you gave it.

> If you only want the AI experience, the separate **AI Pack** download is the
> same documents in a flat folder with no subdirectories — easier to drag into
> a chat window in one go.

### ★ The minimum upload

`AEONISK_PRIMER.md`, `Aeonisk - YAGS Module`, `Aeonisk - System Neutral Lore`,
`NEXUS_LAW.md`, `FACTION_REFERENCE.md`, `DM_SYSTEM_PROMPT.md`.

Add the Economy, Gear, and Culinary references when you want shopping,
loadouts, or a scene in a restaurant to have real numbers behind it.

### Canon precedence

Where two documents disagree, the later entry wins:

1. `NEXUS_LAW.md` — the Sovereign Nexus Constitution. Governs where its wording
   differs from the Module's.
2. `FACTION_REFERENCE.md` — **locked.** The authority on the eight factions.
3. `Aeonisk - System Neutral Lore` and `Aeonisk - YAGS Module`.
4. Everything in `04-reference/` — texture, and safe to overrule at your table.

The Tactical Module is marked *playtest* and is an optional layer. Nothing else
depends on it.

## Running it at a table instead

It works fine with a human GM — it's a complete YAGS module. One roll carries
the game: **Attribute × Skill + d20 vs. a difficulty.** Eight attributes, skills
rated 0–8, and unskilled attempts halve the die, so competence is real and its
absence is really felt.

What you'll want to internalize before session one: **Soulcredit** (−10 to +10,
private until a gatekeeper tests it), **Void** (0 to 10, warps reality at 5+),
and **Bonds** (three at most; they grant real mechanical strength and can be
sacrificed for terrible power).

## Non-negotiables, for humans and machines alike

- **Eight factions, no more.** Sovereign Nexus, Pantheon Security, Astral
  Commerce Group, Arcane Genetics, House of Vox, Aether Dynamics, Tempest
  Industries, Freeborn.
- **ACG ≠ ArcGen.** Astral Commerce Group is debt. Arcane Genetics is biocreche.
- **The currency ladder is 20× per tier.** 1 Drip = 20 Breath, 1 Grain = 20
  Drip, 1 Spark = 20 Grain. Markets float ±20–30%; the ladder does not.
- **Soulcredit is private** until something tests it.
- **Don't fabricate canonical families, events, artifacts, or institutions.**
  Saying "I don't know" is in-genre — the Codex's own memory is lossy, and that
  is the setting's engine of mystery, not a flaw in it.
